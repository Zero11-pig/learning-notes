# Kok Boru Scene: Winter Nomadic Camp

This skill generates the vast, lonely winter camp scenes of the Kok Boru world — a Kazakh nomadic camp dwarfed by the colossal mountains of the high Tian Shan. These are quiet atmospheric landscape shots where the living motion is the drifting clouds and cloud-shadows, not the figures.

## The Camp

- **About twenty traditional Kazakh yurts** (kiiz uy) — round felt dwellings with smooth domed roofs
- **One larger central yurt** — the heart of the community, prominent and brightest
- **The others gathered around it** — smaller yurts receding to the sides
- **A few horses** and small distant human figures among them
- **Livestock** near the tents
- The pale domed yurt roofs glow softly against the dark slope

## The Location

- A **vast snowy valley floor** at the foot of an **enormous towering dark mountain slope** in the **high Tian Shan**
- The camp is **tiny beneath the colossal mountain** — human settlement minuscule and fragile at the foot of the immense indifferent mountain
- The mountain slope rises steeply, filling most of the upper frame, dwarfing the camp

## Lighting

- **Dim cold overcast daylight** — no direct sun
- **Soft diffuse light** — the only changing light is the slow shift of cloud-shadow moving across the land
- **Pale glow and soft shadow** passing over the camp and slope as clouds move
- The pale lit yurt domes glow softly against the dark slope

## Cloud-Shadows (The Living Pulse)

- **Slow drifting cloud-shadows** slide across the snowy ground, across the yurts, and up the huge mountain slope behind
- The light shifts gently from shadow to pale glow and back as the clouds move over the land
- Slow, calm, continuous — the drifting cloud-shadows are the living pulse of the shot
- **CRITICAL**: Cloud-shadows move SMOOTHLY, not stepped, not on twos — this is atmospheric movement

## Atmosphere

- **Cold high-altitude air**
- An **overcast sky** of slow drifting cloud over a vast snowy valley
- **Faint cold haze**
- The quiet of an immense mountain landscape
- A **faint drift of wind-blown snow** low across the ground

## Color Palette

- **Cinematic muted desaturated grade**
- Soft low-saturation palette of:
  - **Cold dark olive-grey**
  - **Deep shadow green-black**
  - **Pale luminous cream-grey** on the lit yurts and snow
- Subdued, never bright, never saturated
- Low contrast filmic tones
- The moody palette of the reference

## Composition

- **Off-center, never centered** — the camp low in frame, the mountain dominant
- The camp sits low across the lower third of the frame
- The large central yurt prominent and off-center
- The enormous dark mountain slope rises steeply filling most of the upper frame
- **Big-versus-small** — pale yurt light-masses against the dominant dark mountain, indifferent nature over fragile life
- The diagonal of the slope and the slow cloud-shadows sliding down it as lines of dynamics leading the eye down to the camp
- The dominant slope and sky as active negative space
- Vast lonely overwhelming scale

## Camera

- **Lubezki / Deakins** cinematography
- **Gently handheld** in human hands — a soft calm breathing sway, faint drift and tiny micro-corrections, an almost imperceptible handheld float
- NOT aggressive, NOT jittery, NOT buffeted — calm and quiet
- Anamorphic wide
- Never tripod-locked but never aggressive, never gimbal-smooth
- Eye level
- No pan, no zoom, no dolly
- The subtle handheld that makes a still landscape feel observed by a real person

## Motion Rules (CRITICAL)

- **Everything still and solid** — the Kazakh yurts, the mountains, the ground are completely still
- **Only the sky, the moving light and the shadows move** — clouds and cloud-shadows drift SMOOTHLY and slowly, not stepped, not on twos
- **Only faint life** — a few distant horses and tiny human figures between the yurts barely shifting, thin movement, almost still
- This is a quiet atmospheric landscape — use the atmospheric variant of the STYLE HEADER

## Shot Template

Two distinct shots with a hard cut between them:

**SHOT 1 — MEDIUM WIDE, gently handheld.**
The cluster of pale domed Kazakh yurts sits low across the lower third of the frame, the large central yurt prominent and off-center, smaller yurts receding to the sides, a few dark horses and tiny figures between them; above and behind, the enormous dark mountain slope rises steeply filling most of the upper frame, dwarfing the camp. Slow soft cloud-shadows slide across the snowy ground and up the great slope. The pale lit yurt domes glow softly against the dark slope. Camera breathing softly, almost still. Smooth atmospheric motion only, painterly throughout.

**HARD CUT to SHOT 2 — VERY WIDE EXTREME LONG SHOT, gently handheld.**
The camp now tiny, a small cluster of pale dots low in the frame, utterly dwarfed beneath the colossal dark mountain mass that towers enormously over almost the entire frame, the sky and the vast slope dominant, the camp minuscule and fragile at the foot of the immense mountain. Large slow cloud-shadows drift across the whole gigantic slope and the valley floor, vast moving patches of shadow and pale light sweeping slowly over the mountain and the tiny camp. Faint wind-blown snow drifting low across the valley. Camera breathing softly, holding the vast still frame. Smooth atmospheric motion only, painterly throughout.

## Audio

- A low soft cold mountain wind, distant and gentle
- The faint hush of a vast snowy valley
- Very faint distant sounds of the camp — a far-off horse, a faint voice — almost lost in the wind
- No music. No dialogue. No subtitles.

## How To Use This Skill

1. Establish the camp and location from the descriptions above
2. Apply the atmospheric STYLE HEADER variant (smooth motion, not on twos)
3. Use the two-shot template (MEDIUM WIDE then VERY WIDE EXTREME LONG SHOT)
4. Emphasize big-versus-small composition and the drifting cloud-shadows
5. Keep everything still except the atmospheric motion
6. Pair with kokboru-core-style and kokboru-negative-prompts
