# Kok Boru Scene: Dream Sequence

This skill generates the surreal vision sequences of Kok Boru — dreams that obey dream logic, not physical logic. The normal cinematography rules of the film are suspended. The signature dream image is a lone white yurt on a flat snowy steppe, surrounded by ~20 dark horses running in a synchronized orbital circle, with fire burning at the yurt's base.

## Dream Logic (CRITICAL)

- **THIS IS A DREAM** — the normal cinematography rules of this film are suspended
- This scene obeys **dream logic, not physical logic**
- The synchronization of the horses is **intentional and correct** — it is the wrongness that tells you this is a dream
- The camera stillness is **intentional and correct**
- The lack of wind is **intentional and correct**
- Nothing here resolves or concludes — the yurt does not collapse, the fire does not spread, it simply burns, steady and permanent, the way things burn in dreams
- The fire's warm light is the only warm light — it is real and present and the only color

## The Scene

- A **lone white yurt** centered on flat snowy steppe — lone, white, small relative to the horses surrounding it
- A herd of **~20 dark horses** running in tight orbital formation around it — a ring, an orbit, NOT a herd running in panic, NOT animals fleeing
- **Fire burning at the yurt's base and doorway** — visible as a hot red-orange glow
- **Dark smoke rising and spiraling** into the vortex of the circle
- **Deep grey sky pressing down**
- **Pale white steppe dissolving at every edge** into cold void
- There is **no horizon** — sky and snow merge into the same pale nothing at distance, the world ending just beyond the circle

## The Horses

- **Approximately 20** — all dark, near-black, coal and charcoal
- **Manes and tails streaming** with the speed of the gallop, NOT wind (there is no wind here)
- They run in a **continuous tight circle** around the yurt
- They **do not break the circle**. They **cannot stop**
- Their motion is **synchronized** — each horse at the same phase of stride as the one beside it, the whole circle moving as one repeating form, a dark ripple going around and around the yurt
- This synchronization is **wrong** — it is the wrongness that tells you this is a dream
- Their hooves carve the circular track deeper with each pass — a dark groove in the white surface, worn down to bare earth in places
- They **do not look at the fire**
- They **do not look inward at all** — their heads are forward, their eyes fixed on the path ahead of them, the circle that has no beginning and no end

## The Fire

- The fire at the yurt's base and doorway is the **ONLY warm light source** in the entire scene
- The **only red, the only orange, the only heat**
- It glows at the base of the yurt and through the open doorway, spilling a faint red-amber wash onto the snow directly around the structure — the only warm patch in an entirely cold world
- **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN**: applying any warm tone to the horses or the steppe outside the immediate fire glow radius
- The yurt does not collapse. The fire does not spread. It simply burns, steady and permanent

## The Smoke

- Dark smoke pouring from the crown of the yurt
- Rises **straight up** into the still air, then begins to **spiral** — drawn into the vortex of the horses' circular motion
- The column bends and curls as the herd passes, then straightens again in the gap
- The smoke is the only thing that knows the horses are there

## Color Palette

- **Cold grey, near-black, and white** — the fire at center the only warm color anywhere
- Everything outside the small fire glow radius: **flat cold grey**, the light coming from the snow itself, rising from the ground
- The sky above is **uniform dark grey pressing down**
- The smoke is **black-grey and heavy**
- The horses are **dark shapes against white** — no warm tones on them, no amber glow on their coats
- The fire draws the eye because it is the only color in the scene

## Atmosphere

- **The dream has no weather** — the wind that governs every waking scene does not exist here
- **Absolute stillness** — the stillness of a place outside time
- **No spindrift, no branch movement, no breath vapor** — or if breath vapor exists, it rises straight up from the horses and hangs suspended without dispersing, columns of grey accumulating above the running circle like smoke that has forgotten to move
- The snow is undisturbed everywhere except the deep circular track carved by the horses' repeated passes

## Camera

- **Elevated — god's-eye angle** — the camera looks down at the scene from above and to the side, a position no living person could occupy
- Wide anamorphic approximately 24mm
- The frame is held with an **eerie, unnatural stillness** — NOT the restless handheld of the waking world, but the **locked, unblinking gaze of a vision**
- The horizon is level. The camera does not shake. It watches. It does not need to. Nothing here can hurt it.
- **Shallow depth of field** — the yurt and the nearest horses in sharp focus, the outer ring of the circle softening, the far flat steppe dissolving entirely into pale grey void
- No ground level. No proximity to danger. Distance is the point — you are watching this from somewhere you should not be

## Shot Template

Two shots with a hard cut:

**SHOT 1 — WIDE, elevated, held still — the vision. (~8 seconds)**
The full circle in frame from above. The yurt centered — in this dream the yurt is the center of everything and the composition reflects this. The horses form their dark orbital ring around it. The circular track in the snow is visible as a dark groove. The fire glows at the center. The smoke rises and spirals. The camera does not move. It watches. The horses run. The circle turns. The smoke curls. The steppe dissolves into grey at every edge. There is no horizon. Only the painterly boil — the brushstrokes alive frame to frame — reminds us this is a drawn world, not a photograph. That aliveness in the stillness is part of what is wrong about this place.

**HARD CUT to SHOT 2 — GROUND LEVEL, inside the circle — close. (~7 seconds)**
The dream shifts perspective without warning. Now we are inside the circle, at ground level, the camera placed between the orbital path of the horses and the burning yurt. The horses stream past in both directions of peripheral vision — dark shapes thundering past left and right simultaneously, the circle visible as two converging lines of animals. The yurt fills the right two-thirds of frame, close now — the fire at its base visible directly, the red-orange glow hot and real. The smoke pours from the crown directly above. The camera is also still — the locked unblinking gaze of the dream, even at this proximity. The horses thunder past without the camera flinching. The fire burns without the camera pulling back. We are inside the circle. We do not know how we got here. There is no way out.

## Audio

- **No music**
- SFX only: hoofbeats in their circular rhythm, the low steady crackle of the fire, absolute silence underneath both
- No dialogue. No subtitles.

## How To Use This Skill

1. Establish the dream logic — state clearly THIS IS A DREAM, dream logic not physical logic
2. Describe the lone white yurt, the synchronized horses, and the fire
3. Apply the elevated god's-eye camera with locked stillness
4. Enforce the color rule: cold grey/near-black/white, fire the ONLY warm color
5. Enforce the no-wind, absolute stillness atmosphere
6. Use the two-shot template (elevated wide then ground level inside the circle)
7. Pair with kokboru-core-style and kokboru-negative-prompts
