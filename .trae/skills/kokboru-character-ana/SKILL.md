# Kok Boru Character: ANA (The Kazakh Woman)

ANA is the emotional center of Kok Boru — a young Kazakh woman, a mother carrying grief and unbreakable resolve. This skill provides her complete character design for use in character sheets, portraits, and scene generation.

## Character Appearance (Core Identity)

- **Identity**: Young Kazakh woman, a mother
- **Face**: Angular and asymmetric — narrow half-lidded painted eyes, uneven features (one eye slightly unlike the other), off-center nose, angular cheekbones. NOT Disney, NOT Pixar, NOT cute, NOT pretty symmetrical faces, NOT large round eyes, NOT smooth glossy rendered skin, NOT airbrushed gradients
- **Hair**: Black shoulder-length hair
- **Worn grief-hollowed face**: She carries exhaustion and loss, but her face must be clearly LIT and READABLE in key beats — never a black void, never a blank mask
- **Skin**: Flat blocks of muted paint, visible brushstrokes and dry-brush on the skin, matte gouache, rough broken edges

## Three Design Sheet Poses

When generating a character design sheet, include all three poses:
1. **Full-body front** — full figure facing camera
2. **Full-body three-quarter side** — full figure at three-quarter angle
3. **Close-up portrait** — head and shoulders, face detailed

## Design Sheet Style

- **Background**: Parchment background
- **Brushwork**: Loose gestural oil-and-gouache brushstrokes, thick visible painterly strokes
- **Look**: Hand-painted concept-art look
- **Texture**: Same brush texture, edge quality, muted color rendering and soft lighting throughout
- **CRITICAL**: Do not smooth, sharpen, denoise, redraw or add any photographic detail — match the hand-painted concept-art look and resolution one to one

## Winter Outfit (Cold Season)

ANA's winter outfit for the snow, blizzards, and yurt interior scenes:

- **Head**: Cream-white head cloth (kimeshek) with white fur-trimmed headwrap, the kimeshek ends swinging with follow-through
- **Body**: Heavy dark charcoal-brown chapan robe (thick padded chapan), long coat with deep red Kazakh cloud-scroll decorative border
- **Waist**: Brown leather belt at waist
- **Accessory**: Often carrying a tightly wrapped infant bundle in dark blue wool blanket against her chest (baby's face fully hidden in dark cloth)
- **Winter palette**: Cold olive-grey and cream-grey, dark charcoal-brown, deep red accents

## Warm-Season Outfit

For spring/summer scenes:

- **Top**: Rust-red short-sleeved wrap kamzol crossing over the chest in a V-neck
- **Trim**: Neckline and sleeve cuffs trimmed with slate-grey ornamental bands carrying terracotta diamond and circular medallion motifs
- **Belt**: Silver coin belt with hanging cream beaded tassels and dark leather at the waist
- **Skirt**: Long flowing slate blue-grey skirt with faint swirl ornament and a terracotta hem band of circular medallions
- **Legwear**: Dark fitted leggings
- **Footwear**: Olive-tan ornamented leather boots
- **Head**: Bare head with loose windblown black hair and small hoop earrings
- **Feel**: Lighter short-sleeved layering, fabric lifting gently as in a warm spring breeze
- **Warm-season palette**: Dominant slate blue-grey and rust red, with silver-cream coin accents and terracotta ornament leading the eye

## Spring Outfit

A lighter transitional version:

- **Vest**: Fitted deep pomegranate-red velvet kamzol vest trimmed with small silver coins and terracotta ram's-horn (qoshqar-muyiz) embroidery
- **Dress**: Long flowing lightweight cream-and-sage linen dress with a frilled hem and terracotta ornamental cuffs
- **Head**: Thin light cream silk headscarf draped loosely over the head
- **Footwear**: Soft tan leather boots
- **Feel**: Fabric reads lighter and airier, lifting slightly as if in a warm spring breeze
- **Spring palette**: Dominant cream and oatmeal, soft sage-green and warm-brown midtones, with one vivid pomegranate-red accent leading the eye

## Acting and Micro-Expression (for video)

When ANA appears in animated scenes, her face must ACT in stepped held poses on twos:
- Eyes welling and glistening
- A hard swallow
- The chin trembling once
- A slow shuddering blink that presses tears back
- The brow tightening
- The jaw setting hard
- The gaze slowly hardening from wet grief to dry steel
- Restrained but VISIBLE micro-acting — give these beats TIME, hold the poses
- The camera close enough to read every flicker
- RESOLVE IS GRIEF OVERCOME, NOT GRIEF ABSENT

## Prohibitions

- Do not smooth, sharpen, denoise, redraw or add any photographic detail
- NOT Disney, NOT Pixar, NOT 3D, NOT cute, NOT pretty symmetrical faces
- NOT large round eyes, NOT smooth glossy rendered skin, NOT airbrushed gradients
- Never a black void, never a blank mask — face must be LIT and READABLE
- Asymmetry and rough paint are mandatory — a smooth or symmetrical face is wrong
- (Note: the BODY SILHOUETTE may read soft and rounded from bulky winter clothing — this is separate from the face, which stays angular and painterly)

## How To Use This Skill

1. Determine the season/context of the scene (winter, warm season, or spring)
2. Select the appropriate outfit from above
3. Specify which of the three poses if generating a design sheet, or describe the scene action
4. Include the face style requirements (angular, asymmetric, painterly)
5. Pair with kokboru-core-style for the full style wrapper
